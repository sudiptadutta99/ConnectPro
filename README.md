# 🚀 **ConnectPro - Your Professional Networking Hub**  

**LIVE AT**: NOT YET... ** 

**ConnectPro** is a dynamic professional networking platform designed to connect individuals, foster career growth, and facilitate seamless communication. Whether you're looking for job opportunities, networking with industry professionals, or sharing insights, **ConnectPro** has you covered!  

---

## 🌟 **Features**  
- 🔹 **Professional Profiles** – Showcase your skills, experience, and achievements  
- 🔹 **Job Listings & Applications** – Find job opportunities or post openings  
- 🔹 **Networking & Connections** – Connect with professionals across industries  
- 🔹 **Real-time Messaging** – Communicate instantly via **Socket.io**  
- 🔹 **Secure Authentication** – Powered by **JWT & bcrypt**  
- 🔹 **Scalable & Fast Backend** – Built with **Node.js & Express.js**  
- 🔹 **Interactive News Feed** – Share posts, articles, and insights  
- 🔹 **AI-Powered Recommendations** – Personalized job and connection suggestions  

---

## 🛠️ **Tech Stack**  
- **Frontend:** React.js, Tailwind CSS  
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js  
- **Database:** MongoDB  
- **Real-time Communication:** Socket.io  
- **Authentication:** JWT & bcrypt  
- **Cloud Storage:** AWS S3 / Firebase Storage  
- **Deployment:** Vercel / Render / DigitalOcean
